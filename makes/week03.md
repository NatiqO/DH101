### Reflection

In this image, I don’t fully see myself because some of the physical details are off. My eyes,
nose, and mouth look the same as they do in real life, and those features immediately make the
image very recognizable to me. My skin tone is similar, if not exact, and even the small spots on
my face are still there, which makes the photo feel more real and not generated. Those details are
what most people would rely on to identify me, so when you look at first glance, it feels like I
might have actually taken the photo.

It's not much that I don’t see myself in the picture, but my hair looks different from how it
actually is, and the shape of my face feels slightly off. That difference is what creates a
disconnect and the unrealisticness of the image, making the image look like an altered version of
me, leading to the idea of the image being generated by AI. The expression on my face in the
image is also an expression I don't think I would make, showing that small changes can shift how
each and every person is seen, and even when some of the structure may be altered, the main
features remain, allowing the person in the image to still be recognizable.
