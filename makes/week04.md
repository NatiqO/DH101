### Reflection

In this comic, I wanted to tell a very simple, easy story that I thought AI could create
perfectly with no problem whatsoever. The story was a dog looking for his shoe and found it
with a squirrel who mistook it for food, very simple and direct. Then I asked the AI to generate
the images and text. The AI generated them, but then had a very hard time making all of the
images and text the same art style and font. At first, I assumed this would be the easiest part of
the project. Since the story was so simple, I expected the AI to make consistent, matching panels
without much effort. Instead, each image looked slightly different, as if it came from a different
comic. The dog changed shape, the colors shifted, and the text didn’t always match the tone of
the story. This made the comic feel less unified and more AI-generated. This showed me that
while the AI could produce pieces of the comic, it could not maintain the overall picture on its
own. I had to choose which images to keep, how to arrange them, and how to adjust the text so
the comic felt like one and not 5 different comics.
