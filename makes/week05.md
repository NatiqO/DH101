# Week 5 – GIF & Remix Culture
## Make 4: AI GIF

![AI generated looping GIF of an apple eating another apple](../assets/images/ai-gif.gif)

### Reflection

When an image repeats instead of just sitting still, it feels less like a moment and more like
something that’s always happening. In my GIF, the apple eating another apple doesn’t just
happen once; it keeps happening over and over, which makes it feel like a cycle. Because it
loops, there’s no real beginning or end, so it starts to feel kind of inevitable, like the action will
never stop.

The looping also changes how you pay attention to it and how it feels emotionally. At first, it’s
kind of funny and random, an apple eating another apple is just a weird idea. But the more it
repeats, the more uncomfortable it gets. The realism, the lighting, the countertop, and the texture
of the apples make it look like it could be real, but the action itself is impossible, and that
contrast makes it feel a little unsettling.

I can see my own choices in the concept, the decision to make it look realistic, and how I
described the scene in the prompt. I also chose which version to keep and how to cut it so the
loop feels smooth. At the same time, the AI handled everything else: the actual motion, the
biting, and the chewing pattern.

AI definitely complicates authorship. The idea was mine, but the exact look and movement came
from the AI’s training and generation. So it doesn’t feel like it belongs completely to me or
completely to the AI—it’s more like a collaboration between both.
