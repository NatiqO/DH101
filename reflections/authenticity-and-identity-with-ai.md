# Reflection 3: What does authenticity mean when identity is shaped with AI?

## Response

When identity is shaped with AI, authenticity means staying in control of what you choose to express and making sure it reflects your own values and experiences. AI can help with ideas or wording, but it does not decide who you are. What makes something authentic is how you use AI and whether you take time to shape the final result yourself.

This connects to *Hacking the Cis-tem*, which shows how systems and technologies can define people by forcing them into fixed categories :contentReference. In the reading, early computer systems classified people in ways that did not reflect their real identities, which shows that technology is not neutral. In a similar way, AI systems are built on patterns and existing data, which means they can influence how identity is expressed or understood.

Because of this, authenticity is not just about being original, but about being intentional. If someone copies AI outputs without thinking, their identity starts to reflect the system instead of their own perspective. But if they edit, question, and adjust what AI gives them, they stay in control of how they are represented.

## Personal Reflection

Personally, I have noticed that AI can make my work sound better, but sometimes it does not sound like me unless I change it. When I take time to rewrite parts and make sure it matches what I actually think, it feels more authentic. This shows that authenticity in the age of AI is not about avoiding AI, but about making sure your voice is still present.
