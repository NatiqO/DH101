# About Me

## Who I Am

My name is Natiq Onafowokan. I was born and raised in Nigeria until the age of 10, when I moved to Chicago. Growing up in two different environments has shaped how I see the world and how I approach new experiences. At the time of creating this website, I am a first-year student at Denison University, double majoring in Finance and Environmental Studies.

## My Interests

Outside of school, I enjoy playing games and listening to music in my free time. I am also interested in learning new skills, especially creative and technical ones. For example, I have spent time learning 3D modeling using Blender, which has helped me think more about design and how things are created digitally.

## My Relationship to AI

Through this course, I explored how AI connects to creativity, decision-making, and real-world systems. At first, I saw AI mostly as a tool that helps get things done faster. Over time, I started to understand that AI is shaped by human choices and data, and that it can have real impacts beyond just convenience.

## What This Site Shows

This site includes my reflections and makes and work from the course. It shows how my understanding of AI developed over time, especially around topics like creativity, authorship, power, and sustainability.

## Goals for This Project

My original goal for this course/project was to just complete a GE but now I am building something that clearly shows both my work and my growth. I wanted the site to be simple, easy to navigate, and actually represent what I learned throughout the course.
