## Tools I Used

Throughout this course, I used several AI tools including ChatGPT, DALL·E, Midjourney, Pollo AI, ImagineArt, and Sora AI. Each tool played a different role depending on the project. Some were used for generating images, others for helping with writing, and some for troubleshooting technical issues.

---

## How I Used AI

I mainly used AI as a tool to help me generate ideas, test different outputs, and improve my work. For example, I used ChatGPT to help refine prompts, explain concepts, and troubleshoot problems when working on projects like the game or website. I also used AI image generators to create visuals, where I would change prompts multiple times to get different variations and then select the best results.

In most cases, I was not just copying what AI gave me. I would go back and edit, compare outputs, and decide what to keep or change. This was especially important in projects like the image series, where I generated many versions and had to choose which ones actually worked.

---

## What I Controlled

Even though AI generated a lot of the outputs, I was still in control of the main ideas. I decided what each project would be about, what prompts to use, and what direction to take. I also controlled which outputs were used and how they were arranged.

For example, in the game project, I created the story, the structure, and the choices, while AI helped with troubleshooting and giving possible ideas for different paths. In the comic project, I created the story, while AI generated the images that I later selected and arranged.

---

## What AI Did

AI handled tasks like generating images, suggesting text, and producing different variations quickly. It helped speed up the process and made it easier to experiment with ideas.

However, AI did not always give the exact result I wanted. Sometimes the outputs were inconsistent, unrealistic, or not useful. This meant I had to adjust prompts or try again multiple times.

---

## Limits and Problems

One of the biggest limitations I noticed is that AI struggles with consistency and control. For example, in the comic project, it could not keep the same art style across all panels. In the image series, some outputs looked too similar or unrealistic.

Another issue is that AI does not fully understand context or meaning. It generates based on patterns, not real understanding. Because of this, I had to review and edit outputs instead of relying on them completely.

---

## Verification and Responsibility

When using AI for research, I did not rely on a single source. I compared information from different sources and used class materials to confirm what I found. I also recognized that some data, especially around AI’s environmental impact, is based on estimates and not exact numbers.

---

## Reflection

Overall, I used AI as a tool and not as a replacement for my own thinking. It helped me work faster and explore ideas, but I was still responsible for the final decisions. This course showed me that AI is not neutral and that how it is used depends on the choices people make.

Using AI also made me more aware of its limitations, including issues with accuracy, consistency, and hidden impacts like environmental cost and labor. Because of this, I now try to use AI more intentionally and think more critically about when and how I use it.
