## Accessibility

I have tried to make my website more accessible in a few different ways. First, I used AI tools to help identify and fix smaller issues on my site. For example, after previewing my website, I used AI to catch small mistakes and improve overall clarity.

I also adjusted the color choices on my website after learning that some combinations had contrast issues. Fixing this helps make the text easier to read for users.

In addition, I added one of my makes directly to the website to make the content more complete and easier to access. I also updated parts of my code, such as fixing a device dependent event handler, to make sure the site works more consistently across different devices.

Overall, these changes helped improve the usability and accessibility of my site, even though there are still areas I could continue to improve.
